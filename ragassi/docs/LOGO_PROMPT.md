# Adaptação transparente da marca

Modo: ferramenta integrada ImageGen, edição a partir de assets/cartao-original.png. O usuário solicitou recriação/adaptação transparente e integração ao site.
Data: 15/10/2026. A primeira saída tinha transparência excessiva e foi descartada. Esta é a segunda saída, inspecionada no cabeçalho sobre navy.
Não se trata do arquivo oficial vetorial da marca; validar com o cliente antes da publicação.

## Prompt final enviado à ferramenta

Create a clean flat restoration of ONLY the upper-left Ragassi workshop logo in this reference card. Output a wide horizontal PNG with TRUE transparent background. The logo artwork itself MUST be fully opaque with alpha 255: solid pale blue-gray RGB(220,229,242) strokes and letters, not white, not semi-transparent. A thin side-profile car silhouette above the exact bold uppercase word 'RAGASSI', with smaller exact uppercase text 'AUTO MECÂNICA' beneath. Keep the original car outline concept and familiar simple automotive lettering, improve crispness at high resolution. Put the logo in the center of a tightly framed 2.3:1 canvas, about 90% width with minimal 5% padding. Plain flat solid light-blue-gray linework and typography, without any stroke outlines around the letters. The inside of the wordmark letters should be solid opaque fill. Everything outside the logo strokes and letter shapes must be alpha 0. NO black plate, no black shape, no square, no white plate, no paper texture, no grunge, no speckles, no distress, no shadows, no gradients, no metallic/beveled contours, no low-opacity logo, no ghosting, no checkerboard pixels. Do not include any other material from the card: no phone, no service list, no red elements, no scheduling strip, no WhatsApp or card logos. Legibility of 'RAGASSI' and 'AUTO MECÂNICA' when placed on dark navy is essential.
